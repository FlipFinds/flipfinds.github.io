For Mercari sellers, shipping choices can change both the cost to fulfill a sale and the fee base used to estimate proceeds. FlipFinds lets you keep the purchase record while you research and price the item.

## Compare sale price and shipping separately

The [Mercari calculator](/calculator/mercari-profit/) uses the sourced US 10% seller fee on item price plus buyer-paid shipping. Enter the shipping you pay, supplies and other expenses separately. Discounts, special programs and marketplace changes can alter the actual result.

A hypothetical $40 sale with $8 shipping collected from the buyer has a $48 fee base. At 10%, that is $4.80. If your shipping costs $8, item cost is $10 and supplies cost $0.75, estimated profit is $24.45 before other costs. The shipping collected is revenue, but the label remains an expense.

## Keep researched details with the item

Use manual or supported barcode/photo-assisted entry, verify the result, and save the actual purchase cost. Research coverage varies by item. Suggested identity or prices do not establish condition, authenticity or demand.

As the item moves from inventory to a sale, keep its status and actual outcome current. Reconcile fees and labels from the completed transaction rather than treating the original estimate as the final result.

## Start with one transaction

Try the fee estimate with an item you know, then record it in FlipFinds. The app supports Mercari sellers' inventory workflow; it does not directly publish or synchronize Mercari listings.

Check the marketplace's current fee information before relying on a preset for a different country or seller program.
