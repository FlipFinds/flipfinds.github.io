eBay research often spans several different tasks: identify an item, find comparable completed sales, estimate the costs of shipping it, and decide what you can pay. FlipFinds helps you keep the item and its economics together as you move through that workflow.

## Research the exact item, then estimate proceeds

Use an identifier or clear title and compare the model, condition and accessories. FlipFinds' eBay sold-comps action opens external research. An active listing is competition; it is not evidence that your item sold at that price.

The [eBay calculator](/calculator/ebay-profit/) includes a common US non-Store fee assumption, buyer-paid shipping and entered marketplace-collected tax in its fee base. Category rates, Store plans and promoted listing charges can differ. Check your own schedule and record extra costs.

## Keep planning and selling connected

Save the purchase cost when the item enters inventory. Update the item's status as you list and sell it, then reconcile the result with your actual selling statement. Keep packaging, shipping and any other relevant expense separate so a large sale is not mistaken for a large profit.

For a hypothetical $40 item bought for $10, $5 seller shipping and $0.75 packaging leave $18.41 under the common preset before ads and other unentered charges. A lower accepted offer can quickly change that result.

## Start without changing your selling platform

FlipFinds is a companion inventory and research workflow. It does not publish eBay listings or automatically reconcile your account. Use the calculator first, then add one researched item in the app.
