You can use FlipFinds locally without a Cloud Workspace account. If you connected Cloud Workspace, you can request deletion of that FlipFinds account and its cloud data here, even if you no longer have the app installed.

## Request deletion

Email [support@parasdigital.net](mailto:support@parasdigital.net?subject=FlipFinds%20Cloud%20Workspace%20account%20deletion) with the subject **FlipFinds Cloud Workspace account deletion**. Include the email address you used to sign in, if you know it. If you used Apple's Hide My Email, mention that; we will help identify the correct account. Do not send a password, sign-in code, or payment details.

We will verify that you control the account, tell you when to expect completion, and confirm when the request is complete.

## What deletion means

Deletion removes your FlipFinds Cloud Workspace account and associated cloud inventory and backed-up photos. It does not delete your Google, Apple, or eBay account. Items and original photos saved only on your phone are outside the cloud account and remain on that device.

Deleting Cloud Workspace does not cancel a Pro subscription. Cancel an active subscription in the App Store or Google Play if you no longer want to be billed.
