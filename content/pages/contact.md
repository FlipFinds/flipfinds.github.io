## Contact FlipFinds

Email support: [support@parasdigital.net](mailto:support@parasdigital.net)

Include your platform, app version and the steps that led to the problem. For account questions, include your support identifier if available. Send only the information needed to investigate; do not send passwords or payment-card details.

For Cloud issues, describe inventory synchronization and photo-backup status separately. A screenshot can help if it does not expose private item or account information.
