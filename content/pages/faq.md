## Getting started

### Do I need an account?

You can use local inventory in the mobile app without an account. Sign in to a Cloud account when you want to connect your inventory across devices. Cloud sends connected inventory data to our Cloud services.

### Where can I download FlipFinds?

FlipFinds is available for iPhone and Android. [Choose your app store](/download/), then follow the [first-item walkthrough](/pages/start-here/) to get started.

## Pro and your account

### What is included in Free and Pro?

See the [Free and Pro overview](/pricing/) and the plan details in the app for available features. Review the price, billing period and renewal terms before confirming a subscription; prices can vary by region and purchase platform.

### How do I manage my subscription?

Use the subscription management option for the place where you purchased. Purchases through Apple or Google are managed through that store's subscription settings. Canceling renewal does not end access before the current paid period expires.

## Inventory and tools

### Will my photos appear on another device?

Inventory sync and photo backup have separate statuses. Sign in with the same Cloud account on each device and check Settings to confirm sync and photo backup have completed. A photo stored only on one device may not be available elsewhere.

### Does FlipFinds show sold comparables or publish listings?

Use marketplace websites to research comparable completed sales, then enter your chosen asking price in FlipFinds. FlipFinds tracks your item records and sales; it does not currently publish or synchronize marketplace listings.

### Can I use the calculators without installing the app?

Yes. The [free calculators](/tools/) work in your browser. Results depend on your costs and editable fee assumptions. Calculator results are not automatically transferred to your inventory.

### How is profit calculated?

Recorded profit is based on your sale proceeds less the recorded purchase costs, fees and shipping. Missing costs can make a result incomplete. Estimates for unsold items stay separate from recorded sales.
