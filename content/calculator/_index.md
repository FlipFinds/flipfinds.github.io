Use this calculator to estimate resale profit before you source, price, or list an item.

## Start with the sale and its costs

Enter item cost and expected sale price, including zero item cost for a free find. Select a marketplace preset or enter Custom fees. Add seller shipping, buyer-paid shipping, packaging and other costs separately. Estimates use US dollars.

## Example and definitions

A hypothetical $40 sale with $10 item cost, $5 shipping and $0.75 packaging produces $20.25 profit using a flat 10% seller fee and no fixed charge. ROI compares profit with cash invested; margin compares it with revenue. Zero investment has no defined ROI percentage.

Use your own profit or ROI target to compare the estimate. The tool does not predict demand or recommend a purchase. [Read the full method and marketplace sources](/resources/fee-assumptions/), or [choose a dedicated tool](/tools/) for maximum buy cost, break-even or a specific marketplace.
