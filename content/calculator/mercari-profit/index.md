## Buyer-paid shipping still affects the fee

The US preset uses a 10% seller fee on item price plus buyer-paid shipping for listings using Mercari's fee structure effective January 6, 2025. There is no separate processing fee in this preset. Check your listing's applicable terms instead of assuming all older transactions used the same model.

Enter shipping received from the buyer and the label cost you pay separately. Shipping receipts are revenue in this calculation, but the label remains an expense; neither should disappear from the estimate.

## Hypothetical shipped item

A $30 sale with $5 buyer-paid shipping has a $3.50 modeled fee. With $8 item cost, a $5 label and $0.50 packaging, estimated profit is $18.00. Excluding the buyer-paid shipping from the fee base would overstate that result.

## Include other charges when applicable

Review [Mercari's official fee guidance](https://www.mercari.com/us/help_center/article/169/). Add applicable transfer, return, shipping adjustment or other charges yourself. Compare the calculator with the actual transaction record after the sale.

## Mercari fee examples

These USD examples use the 10% preset and $5 buyer-paid shipping. Proceeds here mean item price plus shipping received minus the modeled seller fee, **before** your item, label, packaging and other expenses.

| Item price | Shipping received | Modeled seller fee | Proceeds before expenses |
|---|---|---|---|
| $30 | $5 | $3.50 | $31.50 |
| $50 | $5 | $5.50 | $49.50 |
| $100 | $5 | $10.50 | $94.50 |

### Does Mercari charge the seller fee on shipping?

Under this US preset, yes: the fee base includes item price and buyer-paid shipping. See [Mercari's fee structure](https://www.mercari.com/us/help_center/article/2517/). Enter your actual label cost separately; shipping rates and adjustments can vary.

### What if I offer free shipping?

Enter zero for shipping charged to the buyer and enter the label you pay as shipping cost. A $30 sale with no shipping collected has a $3 modeled fee. With $8 item cost, a $5 label and $0.50 packaging, estimated profit is $13.50.

### Is this my final payout?

No. This estimate excludes unentered adjustments, returns, transfer charges and other expenses. Blank optional cost fields are treated as zero for planning, not as verified costs. Compare the final transaction statement with your records.
