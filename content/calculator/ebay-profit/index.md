## Check the proceeds of a potential eBay sale

Enter item cost and expected sale price, then shipping, packaging and any additional costs. The eBay preset models the common US non-Store category fee. It includes shipping collected from the buyer and any entered marketplace-collected sales tax in the fee base; tax itself is not seller revenue.

Category rates, Store subscriptions, promoted listings and account charges can differ. Editing the fee fields switches to Custom rather than silently applying eBay's preset rules to an override.

## Hypothetical sale

A $40 item with a $10 purchase cost, $5 seller shipping and $0.75 packaging leaves $18.41 using the preset with no collected tax entered. This excludes ads and other unentered charges. If $3 tax is collected, the fee increases and estimated profit falls to about $18.00.

## Before using the estimate

Match your category and account terms to the [official eBay fee schedule](https://www.ebay.com/help/selling/selling-fees/selling-fees?id=4822). Enter actual additional charges, and check sold comparables before assuming your asking price is achievable. This tool estimates proceeds; it does not value the item or publish a listing.

## What does the eBay fee preset include?

For most US non-Store categories, the preset uses 13.6% of the fee base through $7,500 and 2.35% of the portion above that. It adds $0.30 for orders up to $10 and $0.40 above $10. The fee base includes item price, shipping collected and entered marketplace-collected tax. These rules were checked against [eBay's published schedule](https://www.ebay.com/help/selling/selling-fees/selling-fees?id=4822) on October 19, 2026.

## Compare fees and proceeds

These hypothetical USD examples assume one item, $5 buyer-paid shipping and $0 collected tax. Proceeds are item price plus shipping collected minus the modeled fee, before item cost, labels and supplies.

| Item price | Shipping collected | Modeled fee | Proceeds before expenses |
|---|---|---|---|
| $30 | $5 | $5.16 | $29.84 |
| $50 | $5 | $7.88 | $47.12 |
| $100 | $5 | $14.68 | $90.32 |

### Does eBay charge fees on sales tax?

The preset includes the tax you enter in the fee base, but excludes it from seller revenue. For the $30 item above, adding $2 collected tax increases the modeled fee from $5.16 to $5.43 and reduces proceeds to $29.57, rounded to cents.

### What about Store rates, ads and special categories?

This preset does not cover every category or Store plan. Use Custom for your applicable percentage and fixed fee, and enter additional charges separately. Custom is a flat-rate model; it does not reproduce category tiers automatically. Check your transaction statement before treating the estimate as an actual result.
