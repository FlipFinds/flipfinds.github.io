Your finds on your phone. More room to work on your computer. The web dashboard is included with FlipFinds Pro—one subscription covers both.

## Get connected in three steps

1. **Update FlipFinds on your phone.** Open Settings and sign in with your original FlipFinds account. If you used Apple or Google, keep using that same sign-in method.
2. **Connect Cloud sync.** Review the sync explanation and enable it. Wait for inventory sync to finish. Signing in by itself does not upload local inventory. Check photo backup separately in Settings.
3. **Open [app.flipfinds.net](https://app.flipfinds.net/dashboard) on your computer.** Sign in with the same account. Your connected inventory appears in the dashboard.

Already Pro on mobile? You do not need a second subscription. If access is missing, check that your mobile purchase is active and that both devices use the same account before purchasing again.

## Make the bigger screen useful

- **Inventory:** find items, review details and record updates with more room to work.
- **Analytics:** explore recorded sales and profit, including a custom date range.
- **Reports:** prepare reports from your inventory and sales records for printing or export.

Your results depend on the costs and sales you enter. Missing fees or incomplete history can affect the picture.

## Prefer to stay local?

You can keep using local inventory on your phone without creating an account. The web dashboard requires Pro and a Cloud account; inventory only appears there after you choose to sync it.

## Need a hand?

For missing items, first check Cloud sync status on your phone. For missing Pro access, check your original sign-in method and restore an Apple or Google purchase in the mobile app if necessary. [Contact support](/pages/contact/) if you are still stuck. Do not send passwords or payment-card details.
