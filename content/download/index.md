## Your first useful action

Add one item and enter its purchase cost. Check an expected sale price, account for fees and shipping, and save it. Later, record what actually happened when it sells.

You can start with local inventory without an account. Optional Cloud requires sign-in and sends connected data to Cloud services. Feature availability, purchase options, and regional prices appear in the app and store.

If you already tried a web calculator, enter the item details manually in the app. Calculator results are not automatically transferred to your inventory.
