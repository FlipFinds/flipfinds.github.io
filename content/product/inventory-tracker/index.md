FlipFinds keeps an item's purchase cost, asking price, category, storage location and sale outcome in one record. Start on iPhone or Android, then use Pro and connected Cloud inventory when you want to work on a computer.

## Follow one find from purchase to sale

This walkthrough uses one canvas weekender bag, quantity one. The amounts are illustrative, matching the example item screen below; they are not a typical selling result or a suggested asking price.

### 1. Add the item before putting it away

Enter the item manually, or use barcode/photo-assisted entry where available and review the suggested details. Give the bag a recognizable title and record its **$12 purchase price**. Add a category and a location such as **Bin A** so the record points back to a physical item.

| Item detail | Illustrative entry | Why record it? |
|---|---|---|
| Title | Canvas weekender bag | Distinguish it from similar finds |
| Purchase price | $12 | Preserve the amount actually paid |
| List price | $45 | Keep an asking price separate from a completed sale |
| Category | Bags | Group similar inventory |
| Location | Bin A | Find the item when you need it |

The record can hold both the $12 cost and $45 asking price while the bag remains unsold. That asking price does not create $33 of realized profit.

### 2. Keep the record useful while you list

Create the marketplace listing separately. FlipFinds does not automatically publish it or synchronize changes to it. Keep your asking price current in the item record, and update the location if you move the bag to another bin.

Before a sourcing trip, review similar items you already own. Before shipping a sale, use the recorded location to find the item. A saved record helps only if it still describes the stock on your shelf.

### 3. Record what actually happened when it sells

Open the item and choose **Mark Sold**. Enter the actual sold price, selling platform, fees and shipping cost. Check those expenses against the transaction instead of assuming the earlier estimate was correct.

For the example bag, a **$45 sold price**, **$12 purchase cost**, **$6.10 fees** and **$6 shipping** leave **$20.90 after those entered costs**:

**$45 − $12 − $6.10 − $6 = $20.90.**

{{< product-shot src="images/app/item-profit.png" alt="FlipFinds example bag sale showing $45 sold price, $12 cost, $6.10 fees, $6 shipping and $20.90 profit" caption="Actual item screen with example data. The result reflects the entered costs, not every possible business expense." >}}

If a fee or label cost is unknown, keep it unresolved until you can check it. Enter an explicit zero only when a charge does not apply. Packaging, returns and other expenses outside these entries still matter when evaluating the transaction; this example does not establish total business profit.

### 4. Review sold and unsold items separately

The sold bag now has a recorded outcome. Another bag still listed at $45 remains unsold inventory. Review completed sales in [profit and analytics](/product/profit-tracker/), and use the [worked inventory reconciliation guide](/guides/inventory-reconciliation/) to match records, physical stock and expenses.

## Choose your workspace

| Workspace | What you need |
|---|---|
| Local mobile inventory | The iPhone or Android app; no account needed for local inventory |
| Desktop inventory dashboard | Pro, the same signed-in Cloud account and inventory you choose to synchronize |

Signing in alone does not upload your local items. Check inventory sync and photo-backup status separately before relying on another device. Already Pro on mobile? Use your existing Cloud account; a second subscription is not required. Follow the [desktop setup guide](/guides/desktop-dashboard/) for connection steps.

## Start with a manageable batch

Enter five items you can physically find. Check their costs and status, then add the next batch. Local inventory works without an account; optional connected Cloud has separate sign-in and synchronization requirements.

Prefer a workbook first? Download the [free reseller inventory spreadsheet for Excel](/resources/inventory-template/). For a larger workspace, explore the [Pro desktop inventory dashboard](/product/desktop-dashboard/) and its [Cloud setup steps](/guides/desktop-dashboard/).
