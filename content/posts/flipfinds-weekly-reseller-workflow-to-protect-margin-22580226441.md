A weekly FlipFinds review can connect the decision to buy with what happened after the sale. Work from actual records rather than total asking prices.

## Start with recorded sales

Choose a recent period and inspect a few items marked sold. Confirm purchase cost, actual proceeds and relevant expenses. Missing shipping or fees can make a strong-looking result misleading.

Compare the original expected price with the outcome where you retained that context. Look for an explainable difference: a discount, wrong shipping assumption or condition issue. Small samples support questions more readily than confident trends.

## Review unsold inventory separately

Check items still being prepared or listed. Do not count their expected proceeds as earned profit. Choose one practical next step for stock you can find and work on.

Use the free calculator when a new sourcing decision needs a cost estimate. The browser estimate does not automatically transfer into the app; record the actual purchase and subsequent outcome yourself.

## Carry one lesson into the next week

If labels repeatedly cost more than expected, measure packaging before buying similar items. If identity mistakes caused pricing errors, improve the research check. Record the reason for the adjustment.

Analytics depend on entered history. Empty periods, offline use and incomplete costs limit comparisons. Local inventory works without an account; optional Cloud requires its own connected setup.
