A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Before you get started.

Contact support →
Do I need an account?

Local inventory works without an account. Optional Cloud requires sign-in and sends connected inventory data to Cloud services. Photo backup availability and status are separate; check Settings to confirm what is backed up.

Does FlipFinds show sold comps?

Use marketplace websites to check comparable completed sales. Match the model, condition and shipping, then enter your chosen list price in FlipFinds. The current app and dashboard do not include a separate Research workspace.

Can I use the calculators without the app?

Yes. The free web calculators work in your browser. Estimates depend on your inputs and editable assumptions. Calculator amounts are not automatically transferred into the app.

What are the Free and Pro options?

Check the app for current features, regional prices and subscription terms. Read the app options overview before deciding.
