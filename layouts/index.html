Frequently Asked Questions

Does FlipFinds show sold comps?
Use the app’s Sold Comps research path to check comparable completed sales where available. Active listing medians summarize asking prices, not confirmed sale prices. Match model, condition and shipping before using either signal; research coverage can vary.
Who is FlipFinds for?
Resellers and thrifters who want to make faster buy decisions and keep a clean, simple record of inventory and profit.
Do I need an account?
Local inventory works without an account. Optional Cloud requires sign-in and sends connected inventory data to Cloud services. Photo backup availability and status are separate; check Settings to confirm what is backed up.
