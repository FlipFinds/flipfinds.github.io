Frequently asked questions

Does FlipFinds show sold comps?

FlipFinds is designed for fast sourcing decisions first. You can also dig deeper into sold comps when you need more confidence before listing or buying.

Who is FlipFinds for?

Resellers and thrifters who want faster buy decisions, clearer profit math, and a cleaner way to track inventory from source to sale.

Do I need an account?

No. FlipFinds does not require an account for the core workflow, and your tracked data stays local to your device.
