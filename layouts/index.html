Frequently Asked Questions

Does FlipFinds show sold comps?
Yes. While the primary scan gives you an instant active median snapshot for speed, you can deep-dive further any time. Just tap the Sold Comps button in the app to see what similar items have recently sold for on eBay.
Who is FlipFinds for?
Resellers and thrifters who want to make faster buy decisions and keep a clean, simple record of inventory and profit.
Do I need an account?
No. FlipFinds works without an account. Your tracked data is stored locally on your mobile device.
