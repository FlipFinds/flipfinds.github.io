BEFORE YOUR FIRST FIND

A few useful answers.

Need a hand? Contact support.

Do I need an account to get started?

You can start with local inventory without an account. Optional Cloud requires sign-in and sends connected inventory data to Cloud services.

What is included, and what requires Pro?

You can start with the app's local inventory. Pro unlocks additional features. Review the Free and Pro options; available purchase options and regional prices appear in the app and store.
