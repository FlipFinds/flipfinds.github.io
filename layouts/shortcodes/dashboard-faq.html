How do I open the web dashboard?

Select Dashboard in the website header, then sign in with your Cloud account. An active FlipFinds Pro subscription is required to use the dashboard.

I already have Pro on mobile. Do I need another subscription?

No. Use the same Cloud account and sign-in method you use in the mobile app. Your connected inventory and Pro access belong together. If your mobile subscription is not recognized, contact support before purchasing again.
